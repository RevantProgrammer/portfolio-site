About

I first got into tech through block coding on Scratch. I used to build games occasionally on the platform. However, I soon started to like robotics and the idea of being able to integrate software with hardware. This is when I was introduced to programming languages like C++, Python, JavaScript and Java (Took me a while to figure out that they are different).

I aim to continue building projects since I love to keep myself constantly challenged and solve real world problems. Some things I am currently working on include Adaptive Traffic Lights and an AI powered planner. You can find more details on these in the ~/projects/ page.

P.S. I thought it would be kind of cool to have my website in the form of an actual terminal. Feel free to just click on links as you would with a normal site in case you do not like the look/feel :(
